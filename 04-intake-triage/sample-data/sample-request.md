# Sample request (fictional)

Submit this through the intake form to try the agent. The intake log is seeded with *"Add regional sales breakdown to the pipeline dashboard"*, so this request is a deliberate near-duplicate.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Your name | Jordan Lee |
| Your email | jordan.lee@example.com |
| Team | Sales Operations |
| Request title | Show pipeline by region on the sales dashboard |
| Business impact | Affects customers or revenue |
| Needed by | 10 days from today |

**Description:**
> Our regional managers can't see their own pipeline in the new CRM dashboard. They need to filter the pipeline view by region before the Q1 kickoff, where each manager presents their numbers. Right now they export to Excel and rebuild it by hand, which takes each of them about half a day.

## What to look for

- **Priority P1** from the rules: revenue impact and needed within 14 days.
- **Possible duplicate** flagged, even though the wording is different ("pipeline by region" vs "regional sales breakdown").
- **Routed to the reporting owner**, with a reason.
- **Missing information** the team really needs (which field defines a region, and whether access is restricted).
- **The reply never promises a date.**
