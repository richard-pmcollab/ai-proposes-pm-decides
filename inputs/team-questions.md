# Questions to send your team

Use this when you want the team's input instead of filling in [`pm-inputs.md`](templates/pm-inputs.md) on your own. Each answer maps to one section of that file.

## Copy-ready message

> Hi team, I'm using some AI tools to cut down PM admin. Could you answer these by [day]? Short answers are fine. Please use project facts only: no client names or confidential details.
>
> 1. **Your tasks:** For each task you own, how far along is it (% done), when is it due, and is it on track, at risk or blocked? If blocked, what's blocking it?
> 2. **Risks:** What risk or issue worries you most right now? How likely is it (High/Medium/Low), how big would the impact be (High/Medium/Low), and what would reduce it?
> 3. **Last meeting:** What did we decide in our last team meeting, and what did you commit to, by when?
> 4. **Key message:** What's the one thing leadership or the client should hear this week? Is there anything that must stay internal?
> 5. **A request:** Send one real request for the team: what you need, why, who it affects, and by when.
> 6. **Dates:** For the milestone you're responsible for, what's your forecast finish date today? If it has moved, why?
>
> I'll send a separate anonymous form for the retro.

The retro has its own form because it must stay anonymous. Use Microsoft Forms (or similar) set to anonymous with three questions: *What went well? What should we improve? Any ideas?* plus a 1–5 mood score. You need at least 3 responses.

## Where each answer goes

| Question | Section in `pm-inputs.md` | Agent |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Your tasks | Tasks | Agent Team, 01 Weekly Status |
| 2 Risks | Risks and issues | Agent Team (RAID) |
| 3 Last meeting | Meeting | 02 Meeting → Actions |
| 4 Key message | Stakeholder update (Update and Internal only) | 03 Stakeholder Translator |
| 5 A request | Requests | 04 Intake Triage |
| Anonymous retro form | Retro | 05 Retro Synthesizer |
| 6 Dates | Milestones | 06 Daily Slip Alert, Agent Team |

A transcript of your stand-up works better than question 3: paste it straight into the Meeting section.

## Before you send anything to the AI

- **Real names.** Put real client, vendor and colleague names in the *Privacy check* section. The checker refuses to continue while any of them appear elsewhere in the file. Use code names instead (for example "Vendor A").
- **Current dates.** If your team answers with old dates, update them. A due date in the past is reported as overdue.
- **Free AI tier.** The free Gemini tier may use what you send to improve Google's products. Send project facts only.
